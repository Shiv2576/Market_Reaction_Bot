  Instant Telegram signal delivery
  
  #description
  High-conviction whale alerts are pushed to private or public Telegram channels within milliseconds—structured with entry, target, stop-loss, and chain context.

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-ph-binoculars

  Bitcoin whale pattern recognition
  
  #description
  Monitors Mempool.Space for anomalous large transfers (>100 BTC), burst activity, and miner fee spikes that historically precede cross-chain volatility.

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-ph-arrows-down-up

  #title

  #description

  icon: i-ph-gauge

  #description
  Each alert includes a dynamic confidence score based on historical correlation strength, market regime, and concurrent macro activity.
