Welcome to **MRT**, a low-latency, self-hosted trading signal engine that turns Bitcoin whale behavior into actionable cross-chain alpha.

## What is MRT?

MRT (**Market Reaction Trading Bot**) is a privacy-respecting, offline-first system that:

- **Scrapes** real-time large-value Bitcoin transactions from [Mempool.Space](https://mempool.space)
- **Analyzes** sender patterns, fee urgency, and transaction clustering
- **Predicts** short-term price impact on **Ethereum** and **Solana** using historical on-chain response data
- **Delivers** structured trade signals instantly via **Telegram**

No cloud dependencies. No KYC. No fluff—just signals grounded in observable on-chain behavior.
